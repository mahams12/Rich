Introduction

NovexaHub (“NovexaHub,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a digital studio offering website development, software development, UI/UX, branding, digital products, and related services through https://novexahub.live and associated channels.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and safeguard information when you browse our website, contact us, create an account, request services, or otherwise interact with NovexaHub.

By using our website or services, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please discontinue use and contact us with any questions.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with NovexaHub, we may collect:

Information you provide

  • Name, email address, and message details submitted through contact forms or email.
  • WhatsApp or other messaging details you choose to share when enquiring about a project.
  • Account details used for client login or notifications, where you create or access an account.
  • Project requirements, files, feedback, and other materials you send while working with the studio.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, and approximate location derived from network data.
  • Usage data such as pages viewed, referring URLs, timestamps, and interaction patterns on our website.
  • Cookie and similar technology data, as described below.

We do not intentionally collect payment card details through on-site checkout. Project purchases and custom work are typically arranged through direct communication channels such as WhatsApp or email.

How We Use Information

We use personal information for legitimate business purposes, including to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the NovexaHub website and studio services.
  • Respond to enquiries, provide quotes, deliver projects, and offer support.
  • Create and manage client accounts or notification preferences where applicable.
  • Send service-related communications about requests, deliveries, or account activity.
  • Monitor performance, diagnose issues, prevent abuse, and protect our systems and users.
  • Meet legal, accounting, tax, and contractual obligations.
  • Understand how the website is used so we can improve content and experience.

We process information where necessary to perform a contract, pursue legitimate interests that are not overridden by your rights, comply with law, or where you have provided consent (for example, optional marketing communications, where offered).

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. NovexaHub may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working reliably, remember preferences, maintain sessions, understand traffic patterns, and improve performance.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect website features such as login sessions or personalization. Browser help pages typically explain how to manage cookie preferences for your device.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

NovexaHub relies on carefully selected third-party providers to host, operate, secure, and improve our website and services. These may include infrastructure and hosting providers, analytics tools, authentication or database platforms, email delivery tools, messaging channels, and other operational service providers.

Based on our current production setup, this may include providers such as Cloudflare (website hosting and delivery), Firebase / Google Cloud services (for example authentication, storage, and analytics where enabled), and communication channels such as email or WhatsApp that you choose to use when contacting us.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review those policies. NovexaHub does not control every aspect of how independent providers handle data once it is shared with them for the purpose of delivering their services to us.

Information Sharing

NovexaHub does not sell your personal information. We may share information only as needed to operate our business and deliver services, including with:

  • Service providers who help us run the website, communications, analytics, or studio operations.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that store or transmit website and application data.
  • Payment processors where applicable for invoices or commercial transactions arranged outside or alongside the website.
  • Professional advisers such as legal, accounting, or security consultants where necessary.
  • Authorities, courts, or regulators when required by applicable law or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • Business successors in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets.

Where we engage service providers, we expect them to use personal information only to perform services for NovexaHub and in accordance with applicable confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures may include access controls, encrypted transport (HTTPS), provider security features, and internal handling practices appropriate to the nature of the data and our operations.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use strong unique passwords for any accounts and notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access related to NovexaHub services.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy — including project delivery, client support, legitimate business records, contractual needs, legal or accounting requirements, dispute resolution, and security.

Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and the context in which it was collected. When information is no longer needed, we take steps to delete, anonymize, or securely archive it in line with our operational practices and legal obligations.

Your Rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you (including where you live and how you interact with us), you may have rights to:

  • Access personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to legal or contractual retention needs.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request information about how your data is processed.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. Some requests may be limited where law requires or permits us to retain information, or where fulfilling a request would adversely affect others.

Children’s Privacy

NovexaHub’s website and studio services are not intentionally directed toward children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to NovexaHub in a manner that is not permitted, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to review and, where required, delete the information.

External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, tools, portfolios, social platforms, or resources. Those sites are operated by independent parties. NovexaHub is not responsible for their content, security, or privacy practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every third-party site you visit.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices. When we make changes, we will revise the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the website or services after an update constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy, except where applicable law requires additional notice or consent.
